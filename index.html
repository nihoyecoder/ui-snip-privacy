UI Snip

Privacy policy

Effective November 12, 2026. UI Snip, listed as UI Snip Beta in the Chrome Web Store, is a Chrome extension made by Manoj Singh Rawat. For questions, write to manojsinghrawat0003@gmail.com.

UI Snip works inside your browser. It sends nothing unless you click Generate prompt or Enhance with AI. Then it sends only what's listed below, through UI Snip's server, to Google's Gemini.

What UI Snip reads on your computer

It reads only the tab where you click its toolbar button, and only what you point it at:

  • Select Design: the element you pick. It reads its layout, sizes, fonts, colors, spacing, borders, shadows, transitions, image sizes, and structure (tag and class names). It describes text by its purpose and length, never by its words, and takes a screenshot of the element.
  • Capture Motion: the area you draw. With your permission, it records the tab for about five seconds and keeps frames of that area.
  • Inspect Animation: the element you pick. It reads what changes as it animates, its timing settings, and its tag, class names, and size.

UI Snip saves none of this, except what you choose to copy or download. The only thing it keeps in your browser is your AI agreement and your beta access code, so it doesn't ask again. Withdraw agreement, in UI Snip, deletes both. It has no account, analytics, ads, or crash reports.

What's sent when you ask for AI

You clickWhat's sent
Generate prompt, in Capture MotionA storyboard image of the frames, and the code stack you chose
Enhance with AI, in Select DesignThe element's screenshot, the design facts UI Snip wrote, and your stack
Enhance with AI, in Inspect AnimationThe animation report UI Snip wrote, and your stack

Each AI request also carries your beta access code.

Images are sent as JPEG, at most 2,000 pixels on the longest side. The facts and the report are cut to 24,000 characters at most, and everything travels over HTTPS.

Never sent: the page's web address, its cookies, your passwords, your browsing history, or other tabs. A screenshot or storyboard shows whatever is visible in what you chose, so use the AI on public pages only.

What UI Snip's server keeps

The server runs on Cloudflare. It passes your request to Gemini and streams the answer back, without saving either. To stop abuse, it counts AI requests:

  • Per day: it turns your IP address into a code, using a secret key, and counts that code's requests for the day, in US Pacific time. It never stores your IP address. It keeps today's and yesterday's counts, and deletes older ones the next time it counts a request.
  • Per tester: it turns your access code into a code the same way, and counts its requests for the day. It never stores the access code. These counts are kept and deleted like the daily ones.
  • Per minute: Cloudflare's rate limiter uses your IP address to allow 3 requests a minute. UI Snip doesn't store it.
  • Country: it reads the country Cloudflare finds for your IP address, and refuses AI requests from the European Economic Area, Switzerland, and the UK, and from Tor or unknown locations. It doesn't keep the country.

If something fails, the server may log a short error message, which never includes what you sent.

What Google and Cloudflare do with it

UI Snip uses Gemini's free tier. Under Google's terms for it, Google uses what's sent, and the answers, to provide, improve, and develop its products and machine-learning technologies. Human reviewers may read, annotate, and process them.

The terms don't say how long Google keeps free-tier data, and Google's privacy policy also applies. So never use UI Snip's AI on pages with personal, confidential, or sensitive information, such as email, banking, logged-in dashboards, internal tools, or client work.

Cloudflare handles your IP address and the request in order to deliver them, under its privacy policy.

Who can use the AI

You must be 18 or older, and outside the European Economic Area, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom. Google's terms allow only its paid service for users there, and UI Snip uses the free one, so its server refuses AI requests from those countries. During the beta, the Chrome Web Store doesn't offer UI Snip Beta there either.

Your choices

  • Nothing is sent until you click. Without the AI buttons, everything stays on your computer.
  • To remove UI Snip, open chrome://extensions and click Remove. The server's counts expire as described above.
  • The counts hold no name, email, IP address, or access code. Each tester has their own code, so I could tell whose count is whose, but a count only says how many AI requests were made that day. For questions, write to manojsinghrawat0003@gmail.com.
